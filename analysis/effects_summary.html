Surprisal, entropy and flexibility effects on distinctiveness

Five datasets × four analyses · read out of the fitted lmer models in the project's R notebooks · compiled 2026-10-11 00:58

Every number below is the slope of z-scored latent distance on a z-scored predictor, with duration and log frequency partialled out. Positive means more surprising (or higher-entropy) units are more distinct. Modality is coded with articulation as the reference level, so the ac − art column is the modality gap: positive means the effect is steeper in acoustics than in articulation.

Read this first

  • Widening the articulatory window weakens the articulatory effect. Across the 5 corpora with both windows, the articulatory slope moves down in every one (by up to 0.0095), while the acoustic slope barely moves (up to 0.0107) — as it should, since only the articulatory window changed. The ac − art gap therefore widens rather than closing. That is the opposite of what the extended window was proposed to show. The overall effect is stable throughout (largest shift 0.0053, no sign or significance flips), so this is a change in where the effect sits across modalities, not in whether there is one.
  • Which flexibility measure you use barely touches the surprisal effect, but changes the flexibility effect. Across the 5 corpora the overall surprisal slope moves by at most 0.0018 between the two moderators and the modality gap by at most 0.0283, with no sign changes. The phone2 flexibility coefficient, by contrast, changes sign in 3 of 5 (aemri, aemri75anno, aeema). So tables reporting the surprisal effect are robust to this choice; tables reporting flexibility are not, and the paper has to commit to one operationalisation. utils/mi.py predicts exactly this: “whatever coefficient you had on the H version of this predictor will change sign when you swap in −I.”
  • 5 cells are still pending: aemri in phone positional entropy · flexibility = h(art | ac, phone), aemri75anno in phone positional entropy · flexibility = h(art | ac, phone), aeema in phone positional entropy · flexibility = h(art | ac, phone), frmri in phone positional entropy · flexibility = h(art | ac, phone) and beus in phone positional entropy · flexibility = h(art | ac, phone). Those notebooks currently display output from an earlier fit, so this report refuses to read them and marks the row pending rather than repeating stale numbers under a new heading.
  • 29 of 35 fitted models converge cleanly. The exceptions are aemri75anno (392 rows), aeema (448 rows) and beus (2,642 rows) — all in the syllable analysis, the smallest models in the set, and singular under both flexibility measures. Their rows are shaded below; read their estimates as unstable and their standard errors as unreliable. Every phone model converges.
  • The ultrasound modality gap depends on the blind-spot list. All beus models use the 15-phone limited_phones list, with the three rhotics added on 20 Sep. On the entropy model, adding them dropped the ac − art gap from +0.0421 at p < .001 (110,877 rows, old list) to roughly a third of that; it now stands at +0.0173 (p = 0.00115, 115,773 rows). A result that moves that much when three segments are reclassified is worth settling deliberately rather than inheriting.

4 · Provenance

Built by analysis/build_report.py, which reads the stored outputs of the executed notebooks. Nothing is refitted, so these are the same fits you ran. Every model uses one specification:

dist ~ PREDICTOR * modality * u2_flexibility * u1_flexibility
     + u2_duration + u1_duration + u2_freq + u1_freq
     + (1 | u1) + (1 | u2) + (1 | word) + (1 | bigram) + (1 | subject)

PREDICTOR is bigram_surprisal or phone2_pos_entropy. dist is z-scored within modality (and within dataset where two corpora are stacked); the predictor, both flexibility moderators and log frequency are z-scored. Modality is coded with articulation as the reference level. Duration and frequency enter additively, so they do not appear in any slope reported here. The moderators are the flexibility columns, not H_art_given_ac.

Singular fits: aemri75anno, aeema, beus, aemri75anno, aeema, beus. Pending: Phone positional entropy · flexibility = H(art | ac, phone): aemri; Phone positional entropy · flexibility = H(art | ac, phone): aemri75anno; Phone positional entropy · flexibility = H(art | ac, phone): aeema; Phone positional entropy · flexibility = H(art | ac, phone): frmri; Phone positional entropy · flexibility = H(art | ac, phone): beus. Checked: the surfaces in section 3, evaluated at flex1 = flex2 = 0, reproduce the printed emtrends values in all 70 comparisons, worst discrepancy 5e-05 (the printed values are rounded to four decimals). The aemri row is the combined USC-TIMIT + USC-75 file.
